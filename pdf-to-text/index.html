How to Convert a PDF to Text

Extracting text from a PDF with this tool takes a few seconds and happens entirely on your device:

  1. Add your PDF. Drag a file into the box above or click to select one from your computer or phone (up to 50MB).
  2. Choose your options. Keep all pages or set a page range, and decide whether to preserve line breaks, remove extra spaces, or add page numbers.
  3. Extract the text. Press Extract Text and the readable content appears instantly in the box below.
  4. Copy or download. Copy the text to your clipboard or save it as a clean .txt file.

When You Need a PDF to Text Converter

PDFs are built for sharing and printing, not for editing. Converting one to plain text is the fastest way to reuse its content. People reach for this tool to:

  • Copy quotes, figures, or paragraphs out of reports, invoices, and contracts.
  • Pull text from research papers and ebooks for notes or citations.
  • Move content into a word processor, CMS, or email without retyping it.
  • Feed clean text into other tools — for example, fixing broken line breaks with our Line Break Remover or tidying spacing with the Whitespace Normalizer.
  • Make a document's words accessible to screen readers and searchable on your computer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF to text converter free?

Yes. FreeTextConvert's PDF to Text tool is completely free with no signup, no watermarks, and no limit on how many files you convert.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. All text extraction happens locally in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device, which makes this tool a good fit for private, confidential, or sensitive documents.

Can it extract text from scanned PDFs or images?

Not yet. Scanned PDFs are images with no selectable text layer, so a standard extractor cannot read them. For those files you need a converter with OCR (optical character recognition).

What is the largest PDF I can convert?

You can convert PDF files up to 50MB. Because processing happens on your own device, very large files may take a few seconds depending on your computer's speed.

Can I extract text from only certain pages?

Yes. For multi-page PDFs you can extract all pages or set a specific page range, then copy the result or download it as a .txt file.

Does it work on mobile and in every browser?

Yes. The tool runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet, or phone, including Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari.
